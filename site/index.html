Questions

Will it get in the way of my work?

No. The overlay is click-through everywhere except the cat herself, so every click, tab and text field behind her works normally. She also tucks into a corner automatically during full-screen video, games and presentations.

Will it slow my machine down?

It's built in Rust and Tauri (not Electron) and renders adaptively — 60 FPS only while you're actively playing with her, dropping to 15 when she's calm, ~7 asleep, and essentially nothing when hidden.

Does it really not send anything?

Correct. The only outbound request in the entire app is the update check, and that's a toggle. No analytics library, no crash reporter, no account system.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing breaks and nothing nags you into a corner. The cat keeps living on your desktop with all her movement and interaction; the customisation and productivity features simply lock until you unlock them.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes — a licence covers two machines. Just paste the same key into Settings on each.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. It's Windows 10/11 only today.
